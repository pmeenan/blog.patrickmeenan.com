Hot off of the [Velocity Conference](http://velocityconf.com/) presses, iOS and Android 2.3 agents are now available from the Dulles, VA location on [WebPagetest](http://www.webpagetest.org/).  
  
We have been working with Akamai on their recently [open-source mobitest agents](https://blogs.akamai.com/2012/03/open-sourcing-mobitest.html) and are happy to announce that the agents are now up and running and are also available for people to use for [Private Instances](https://sites.google.com/a/webpagetest.org/docs/private-instances).  
  
Testing is limited to loading URL's and capturing video (and waterfalls) for now with some of the more advanced testing capabilities that you are used to from WebPagetest coming soon.
